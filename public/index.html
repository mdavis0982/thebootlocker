Disclaimer notice

Before purchasing

Please check the listing carefully and ask any questions before arranging payment.

Football boots can deteriorate with age, storage and use. This is particularly relevant to older or rare models, even when a pair is described as new or unused. Their lifespan cannot be guaranteed.

Every listing is described as accurately as possible. Close-up photos or videos of pre-owned pairs are available where needed, so please ask if you want to see anything in more detail.

Sales are final and returns or refunds are not offered. Please make sure you are satisfied with the size, condition and age of the boots before purchasing.

Condition notes are part of the listing. Message @the_boot_locker before paying if anything is unclear.
