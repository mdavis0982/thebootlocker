Disclaimer notice

Hi folks,

Just a few things to take into account before purchasing any pair of boots from @the_boot_locker:

Please be aware that due to age, condition, exclusivity, rarity and other factors, I cannot guarantee the life-span of any pair of boots, whether used or new. Therefore I cannot offer refunds or returns.

I wholeheartedly do my best to describe every single boot as honestly and accurately as possible. This is also the reason that I take closeup videos of every pair that has been pre-worn.

With this in mind, the ugly truth is that there is a possibility that any pair of boots (used or new) could fall apart at any moment... and unfortunately this has been the case on a very small number of occasions. But that is the harsh reality, sadly.

I am a genuine guy who loves running this page, and sorting you folks out with boots of all shapes, colours and sizes. The last thing I want is for anyone to have a bad experience with myself.

But please bear all of this in mind before enquiring about any pair of boots.

Thank you all for your continued support.
